    What is needed for genuine individual-player statistics?

    Stable, automatically verified player identities across the match, plus external validation of ball–player control. Manual identity corrections on the supplied game are not permitted. An external motion-linking experiment did not meet its 99.5% validation precision requirement, so automatic merging remains disabled. Until stable identities are validated, the dashboard preserves anonymous tracks and an unassigned bucket.

    TESTED AWAY FROM THE CHALLENGE MATCH

    External match evidence

    On Fortuna Düsseldorf–St. Pauli, the boundary-aware model made fewer possession misses and fewer false-positive spells than the previous model. The improvement is modest; merging remains a limitation.

    Reference baseline0.361
    Previous model0.486
    Boundary-aware model0.503

    These are clean-tracking possession-segmentation F1 scores, not percentages of match accuracy. With simulated ball dropout, the previous and new models both score about 0.423. This separate test shares a club with training.

    What the numbers mean

    Team possession: each team's control time divided by total live team-control time. Stopped and unknown periods are excluded.

    A possession spell: a continuous interval assigned to one team. Brief passes do not automatically change team possession.

    Player control: currently an anonymous-track proximity estimate, with unassigned time retained.

    Honest limits

    • Model estimates can miss or merge genuine possessions.
    • Offline processing uses future context; this is not a live forecast.
    • External test results do not establish challenge-match accuracy.
    • Challenge-match player identities remain unresolved.
    Reproducibility and competition rules

    Python, frozen external-trained weights and a Jupyter notebook are included in the repository. Training and tuning use licensed external data only. Provided-match predictions are not checked against video or competition ground truth. Match files and generated analyses stay local.
